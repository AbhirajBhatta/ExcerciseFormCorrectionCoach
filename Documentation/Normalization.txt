Why Normalization Is Needed

Right now data is:

(x, y) ∈ [0, 1] relative to image

Problems:

depends on camera distance
depends on person position
not comparable across frames
Example:

Same push-up:

close to camera → large coordinates
far from camera → small coordinates

👉 Without normalization:
angles + motion = WRONG

🎯 What Normalization Does

We enforce:

1️⃣ Translation invariance

Move body to origin

2️⃣ Scale invariance

Normalize body size

3️⃣ (optional later) Rotation invariance
